Every state a card can be in

Rule of this design: the card grows with what happened. Nothing to say means one line; points mean a line plus the problem bars; something to do means a line plus a button. The badge colours are the same three as everywhere — grey (nothing to do yet), amber (you have to act), red (this cost you points).

Not handed in yet deadline open, no file — the card carries the upload button.
Handed in deadline open — the card carries the file and “replace”.
Grace period — 15 min left the amber is the whole point of the state.
Late — your tutor decides accepted still null.
Late — accepted counts after all.
Late — rejected 0 points, card stays collapsed.
Waiting to be marked plus “16 points at stake”.
6.5 / 16 marked — no badge, the bars appear.
Correction there, no points the correction file is downloadable, the bars are not there.
Not handed in 0 of 16 counted towards the term.
