Rule of this design: the points are the status. A marked sheet gets no badge at all, only its number. A badge appears only when there is no number to show, and it comes in three colours — grey (nothing to do yet), amber (you have to act), red (this has cost you points). Green is never used: points already say it.

Late — your tutor decides — handed in after the grace period, accepted is still null.
Late — accepted — the tutor let it count.
Late — rejected — it does not count.
